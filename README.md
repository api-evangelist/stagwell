Stagwell (NASDAQ: STGW) is a digital-first global marketing services and technology company and a Fortune 1000 firm, operating a network of creative, media, communications and research agencies alongside the Stagwell Marketing Cloud (now branded The Marketing Cloud), a suite of proprietary SaaS and DaaS marketing-technology products built by its in-house engineering teams. Stagwell does not publish a unified public developer portal at the holding-company level and ships no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI or agent card of its own; developer access is exposed product by product. The most substantial public developer surface in the group belongs to IMAI / InfluencerMarketing.ai, acquired with LEADERS in July 2024 and folded into the PRophet Comms Tech suite, which publishes a credit-metered REST creator-data API (Discovery and Raw), an llms.txt, and an OAuth-protected Model Context Protocol endpoint on its own host.

**Areas:** Marketing · Advertising · Media · MarTech · Influencer Marketing · Market Research · Creator Economy · Public Relations · Consumer Insights · Holding Company

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Stagwell: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
