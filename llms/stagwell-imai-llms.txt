# IMAI - InfluencerMarketing.ai

> IMAI is an award-winning influencer marketing platform with a database of 400M+ creators across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitch, and more. Brands and agencies use it to discover creators, run campaigns, track ROI, and automate payouts — all from one platform. Trusted by Estee Lauder, Playtika, Lumenis, and Argania. SOC2 Type II certified. 7-day free trial, no commitment.

IMAI operates across 6 workspaces: Influencer Marketing, Consumer Intelligence, PR & Media, UGC Video Ads, LLM Visibility, and AI Agents. It serves enterprise marketing teams, agencies (white-label), eCommerce/DTC brands, and developers via API. Integrations include Shopify, Amazon, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento, Stripe, Klaviyo, Zapier, and Hootsuite.


## Platform

- [Influencer Marketing Workspace](https://influencermarketing.ai/workspace-influencer-marketing/): Find, vet, recruit, brief, and manage creators at scale. Filter 400M+ profiles by audience demographics, engagement rate, follower count, fake follower detection, niche, and 50+ other parameters. Supports bulk outreach with AI-generated personalized briefs.
- [Consumer Intelligence](https://influencermarketing.ai/consumer-intelligence/): Monitor 2 trillion+ social conversations in real time. Identify trends before they peak and brief campaigns directly from live market signals.
- [PR & Media](https://influencermarketing.ai/pr-media/): Access a database of 1M+ journalists and target outlets that are actively cited by AI models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity). Built for earned media in the age of LLM search.
- [UGC Video Ads](https://influencermarketing.ai/ugc-video-ads/): Source UGC content from real creators combined with AI avatar video production, delivered within 14 days.
- [LLM Visibility](https://influencermarketing.ai/llm-visibility/): Track how your brand appears inside AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Optimize for AI search presence.
- [AI Agents](https://influencermarketing.ai/ai-agents/): Deploy 24/7 voice and chat agents trained on your brand and campaign data.
- [Campaign Tracking & ROI](https://influencermarketing.ai/track-performance/): Capture posts, track sales, identify best-performing SKUs, calculate media value, and generate branded reports. Includes Influencer LiveLinks for real-time performance monitoring.
- [Relationship Management](https://influencermarketing.ai/relationship-management/): Manage your full creator roster, communication history, contracts, and payments in one place.
- [Creator Payouts](https://influencermarketing.ai/creator-payouts-2/): Global payment infrastructure for paying creators directly through the platform.
- [Agency White-Label Reporting](https://influencermarketing.ai/agency-reporting-tool/): Agencies can present client-facing dashboards and reports under their own brand.
- [Competitive Analysis](https://influencermarketing.ai/competitive-analysis/): Monitor competitor influencer activity and benchmark your share of creator voice.
- [Influencer Marketing API](https://influencermarketing.ai/api/): Programmatic access to influencer search, data, and campaign workflows for product teams and developers.
- [Find Influencers](https://influencermarketing.ai/find-influencers/): Entry point for creator discovery with guided filters.
